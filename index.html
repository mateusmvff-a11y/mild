Two modes, one switch.

Turn on Kids watching in the corner of the player and three things change at once. The picture, the controls and the rules.

Adults watching

  • One glass bar with everything in reach
  • Hold the video for 2×, slide right for 3× and 4×
  • Chapters on the timeline
  • Dragging the timeline clears the screen
  • Scroll over the volume, double click for full screen
  • Captions, quality, speed and picture in picture
  • Picks up where you left off

Kids watching

  • Color and brightness come down a little
  • Three buttons, each about 2 cm wide
  • Time as a bar that fills, with a dot per chapter
  • No skipping ahead, no speed, a quieter top volume
  • A grown-up picks how long: this video, 10, 20 or 30 minutes
  • The last minute winds down, then the screen rests
  • Playing more takes a grown-up, who types three numbers written as words

Every choice has a reason.

Mild started from what people complain about in players today, and from what parents ask of kids' video.

Big targets for small hands

Nielsen Norman Group tested children from 3 to 12 and recommends touch targets of at least 2 cm. The Kids buttons are 76 px, about four times the adult guideline.

An ending that ends

Autoplay is the feature parents fight most. In Kids mode nothing starts by itself. The video winds down, stops, and waits for an adult.

Glass you can still read

Translucent players got harder to read. Mild dims the footage behind its glass first, so white text keeps its contrast over snow or night.

Usable by everyone.

  • Every control works from the keyboard, and every dialog can be left with Escape.
  • Focus is always visible, in both modes.
  • The grown-up gate takes typed numbers, so it works without a mouse.
  • Controls stay legible over bright and dark footage.
  • Targets are 44 px or more on touch screens, 76 px in Kids mode.
  • With reduced motion on, the bar simply appears and the spinner slows.
  • Higher contrast or reduced transparency settings switch the glass to solid.
  • The layout holds from a phone held upright to full screen.

Put it on your site.

Mild is plain HTML and CSS on top of Video.js v10. Start in Kids mode by adding one attribute.

<video-player mode="kids">
  <media-container class="media-skin mild">
    <video src="video.mp4" playsinline></video>
    ...
  </media-container>
</video-player>
